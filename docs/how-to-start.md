# Copilot SkillPath — how to start

A small app that helps you start learning test automation with Copilot. It sets up a Copilot Notebook as your learning workspace and gives you the right prompt for each step. Copilot does the teaching; the app prepares the context.

**About 10 minutes to set up. After that, one prompt per step, whenever you have time.**

## 1. Open the app

Download `copilot-skillpath.html` and double-click it. It opens in your browser.

Nothing to install, and it works offline. Everything you enter stays in your browser, on your computer.

## 2. Pick your stack

On the first screen, choose one:

- **Starter automation: Playwright + JavaScript**
- **Starter automation: Selenium + Java**

Check the details (experience, time available, target), change anything that doesn't match you, and select **Save and start**. You can also build a custom profile.

## 3. Set up your Copilot Notebook

The app walks you through four short screens:

1. **Get your reference files** — download the 3 files. They tell Copilot who you are and what you're learning.
2. **Create your Notebook** — in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app: Notebooks → All notebooks → New notebook. Upload the 3 files under *Add content to References*, then Create.
3. **Tell Copilot who you are** — copy the instructions and paste them in your Notebook under More options (…) → Instructions.
4. **Start your learning plan** — copy the prompt and paste it into the Notebook chat. Copilot creates your plan.

## 4. Follow the steps

Steps 1 to 10 take you from an empty machine to tests running and pushed to GitHub.

For each step: copy the prompt from the app, paste it into **the same Notebook**, and follow Copilot one step at a time. If you get stuck, use **Need help with this step?** for *I'm stuck*, *Explain this*, *Review my work*, or *Check my understanding*.

When a step is done, Copilot asks you a few questions, then sends you back to the app. Select **Mark as done** and copy the next prompt.

## Also in the app

- **Theory** — short chapters on the ideas behind each step, with a *Go deeper in Copilot* prompt for your stack.
- **Quizzes** — build a quiz: Copilot asks one question at a time and explains every answer.
- **Prompt Library** — extra prompts for planning, practice, and review.

## Good to know

- Always use **the same Notebook**, so Copilot remembers your setup and your progress.
- Your progress is saved in this browser only. Use **My Profile → Export data** for a backup, or if you switch computers.
- Changed your profile? Go through the Notebook setup again and paste the updated instructions.
